01 / THE BEGINNING OF MOST BAD IDEAS
WHAT IF WE…

I've always been drawn to creating things. Not just physical stuff, but experiences.

It started with taking things apart, figuring out how they worked, and then asking that question.

Apparently I'm incapable of leaving it unanswered.

02 / ARTIST? MAYBE.

I NEVER REALLY
DECIDED TO BE AN ARTIST.

I've always made things. Not just physical things—experiences. I take something apart, figure out how it works, and almost immediately start wondering what else it could do.

Most days I still don't really consider myself an artist. I see myself more as a thinker: somebody who gets stuck on an idea and spends an unreasonable amount of time chasing it down just to see what it could become.

I don't do this for visibility, followers or money. It wasn't a path I chose so much as something I eventually realized I had never stopped doing.

LET THE THING BECOME
WHAT IT WANTS TO BE.
03 / WHY I NEED THIS

A PLACE FOR MY MIND
TO BREATHE.

In my day-to-day world, I'm often solving problems that feel almost impossible—problems that have to be solved, for people who may never understand the magnitude of what went into solving them.

You can pour everything into it and watch all that energy disappear into the process.

These projects became somewhere else to put that energy.

A PLACE TO SOLVE A PROBLEM
THAT DOESN'T NEED SOLVING.

To explore the potential of something most people wouldn't give a second thought to. To make all that energy feel alive again.

04 / THE PROCESS

PURE CHAOS.
UNTIL IT BECOMES UNIFORM.

I love the layers. The little details. The things somebody may never consciously notice but somehow still feels.

The more I build into something, the more the chaos starts making sense in its own way.

“WHOA.
THIS ACTUALLY WORKS.”

That's the feeling I'm chasing.

05 / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

MAKE SOMETHING
THAT STOPS YOU.

Something that makes you push a button you probably shouldn't push.

Something that makes a stranger turn to another stranger and say:

“DUDE.
COME FUCK
WITH THIS.”

I don't need them to know my name. I want them to have the moment.

06 / NEXT YEAR

I KEPT SAYING
“NEXT YEAR.”

My family and I look forward to CONNECT every year. Every time we walked the trail, I'd see all that incredible work and tell myself, “Next year, I'm going to build something for this.”

Then life would get busy. I'd look at everybody else's work, convince myself I wasn't really an artist, and another year would pass.

Eventually I realized I was doing the exact thing Time Is an Illusion is about: waiting for the calendar to tell me I was ready.

SO I ASKED ANYWAY.

I missed the deadline. I didn't ask for compensation. I just didn't want another year to go by without putting the thing I made into the world.

07 / THE VOICE IN MY HEAD

I DON'T ALWAYS
BELIEVE I BELONG HERE.

There are still times I look at the work of people around me and wonder how the hell something I made ended up beside theirs.

Part of me still feels like I'm just some dude who likes making weird shit in his garage and somehow wandered into a place he wasn't supposed to be.

I'm nervous. I'm proud. I'm fucking overjoyed. More than anything, I'm grateful.

Grateful that something I made can sit among work from artists I genuinely admire. Grateful that a stranger might stop, touch it, play with it, laugh with their friends and never know a thing about me.

Even when part of me still isn't convinced I've earned that space.

BELIEVING IN YOURSELF
DOESN'T ALWAYS HAVE TO COME
BEFORE DOING THE THING.

Sometimes you do the thing first.

BUILD IT.SUBMIT IT.PUT IT OUT THERE.BE NERVOUS AS FUCK.

Maybe the belief catches up later.

08 / IF ANY OF THIS MATTERS

MAKE THE
FUCKING THING.

If something I make inspires somebody else to finally try the ridiculous idea that's been sitting in their head, that means more to me than whether they remember my name.

You don't have to feel ready. You don't have to call yourself an artist. You don't need permission to make something that probably has no business existing.

LET IT BECOME
WHAT IT WANTS TO BE.

Put it into the world. Maybe somebody else needs to see you try before they're willing to try too.

You can be grateful before you feel deserving. You can be nervous and still show up. You can doubt yourself and still make something worth sharing.
