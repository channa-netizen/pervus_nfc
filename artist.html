HOW IT STARTS

WHAT IF WE…

I've always been drawn to creating things. Not just physical stuff, but experiences.

It usually starts with taking something apart, figuring out how it works, and then wondering what else it could do.

Apparently I'm incapable of leaving that question unanswered.

WHO I AM

I NEVER REALLY DECIDED
TO BE AN ARTIST.

Most days I still don't really consider myself one. I see myself more as a thinker—somebody who gets stuck on an idea and spends an unreasonable amount of time chasing it down just to see what it could become.

I don't do this for visibility, followers or money. It wasn't a path I chose so much as something I eventually realized I had never stopped doing.

In my day-to-day world, I'm often solving problems that feel almost impossible. Problems that have to be solved, for people who may never understand the magnitude of what went into solving them.

You can pour everything into something and watch all that energy disappear into the process.

These projects became somewhere else to put that energy. A place to solve a problem that doesn't need solving. To explore the potential of something most people wouldn't give a second thought to.

A place for my mind to breathe.

WHAT I'M CHASING

PURE CHAOS.
UNTIL IT STARTS TO MAKE SENSE.

I love the layers. The little details. The things somebody may never consciously notice, but somehow still feels.

There's something about adding more and more chaos until, eventually, it becomes uniform in its own weird way.

And then there's that moment when some ridiculous idea that probably had no business existing outside of my head suddenly works.

“Whoa. This actually fucking works.”

That's the feeling I'm chasing.

And when somebody else experiences it, I want them to feel some version of that too. I want to make something that makes you stop. Something that makes you push the button you probably shouldn't push.

Something that makes one stranger turn to another and say:

“DUDE.
COME FUCK
WITH THIS.”

I don't need them to know my name. I want them to have the moment.

THE VOICE IN MY HEAD

I DON'T ALWAYS
BELIEVE I BELONG HERE.

There are still times I look at the work of people around me and wonder how the hell something I made ended up beside theirs.

Part of me still feels like I'm just some dude who likes making weird shit in his garage and somehow wandered into a place he wasn't supposed to be.

I'm nervous. I'm proud. I'm fucking overjoyed. More than anything, I'm grateful.

Grateful for every opportunity. Grateful for every person who stops to interact with something I made. Grateful that these ridiculous ideas get to exist somewhere outside my head.

Even when part of me still isn't convinced I've earned that space.

Believing in yourself doesn't always have to come before doing the thing.

BUILD IT. SUBMIT IT. PUT IT OUT THERE. BE NERVOUS AS FUCK.

Sometimes you do the thing first.
Maybe the belief catches up later.

IF ANY OF THIS MATTERS

MAKE THE
FUCKING THING.

If something I make inspires somebody else to finally try the ridiculous idea that's been sitting in their head, that means more to me than whether they remember my name.

You don't have to feel ready. You don't have to call yourself an artist. You don't need permission.

You can be grateful before you feel deserving. You can be nervous and still show up. You can doubt yourself and still make something worth sharing.

Let the thing become what it wants to be.

Put it into the world.

Maybe somebody else needs to see you try before they're willing to try too.
