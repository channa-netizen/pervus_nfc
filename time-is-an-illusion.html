THE ILLUSION

TIME BECAME
A RULER.

Time is one of the most important things in life, and somehow we're never really taught how to use it. We learn to read clocks. Count days, weeks, months, and years. We organize our lives into calendars and schedules. We remember events by asking how long ago they happened, and plan our futures by asking how much longer until something arrives.

Understanding that sequence matters. It gives us perspective. It helps us learn from the past and prepare for the future.

BUT SOMEWHERE ALONG THE WAY,
WE MISTOOK MEASURING TIME FOR MASTERING IT.

We do this all the time. We say, next week, next month, maybe next year. Next week to start the project. Next month to change something. Next year to finally chase what's been sitting in the back of our mind.

Somewhere along the way, we start giving the clock authority over decisions that were ours to make the whole time.

RIGHT NOW

THE ONLY MOMENT
YOU CAN TOUCH
IS THIS ONE.

Every meaningful decision, every accomplishment, every change we will ever make begins in the present moment. If there is someone you aspire to become, that person isn't created by waiting for the right time. They're created by acting while the time is now.

THE PERFECT MOMENT
RARELY ANNOUNCES ITSELF.

More often, it is made by the decision to begin. Maybe we should spend less time asking what time it is and more time asking what we're doing with the time we have.

TIME ISN'T JUST SOMETHING TO COUNT.
IT'S SOMETHING TO SPEND.

Its value isn't determined by how much of it we possess, but by what we choose to do with the moment in front of us.

WHY THIS EXISTS

A CLOCK THAT
BENDS YOUR PERCEPTION.

I came across a transformed grandfather clock online years ago that stuck with me. The artwork was bold, unique, and beautifully executed, but I couldn't stop thinking about what light could become if it wasn't an accessory.

What if the light became part of the artwork itself?

Eventually, I found an estate-sale grandfather clock and started chasing that question. Thousands of LEDs, holographic foils, movement, custom controls, electronics, code, and an unreasonable amount of tinkering later, it became something much bigger than a clock.

"WHAT IF WE…"

That's usually how this shit starts.

THE PART I LOVE MOST

I HOPE YOU FIND IT
AND I NEVER KNOW.

I hope you stop. Interact with it. Laugh with your friends. Maybe stare at it a little longer than you expected to. I hope you experience something I poured an absolutely ridiculous amount of myself into without knowing anything about the person behind it.

And maybe I never even know it happened.
That's fucking beautiful to me.

There aren't any instructions. There isn't a correct combination of buttons or a right way to experience it. Push something. Change the lights. Find some inspiration.

Every person who interacts with the clock changes it, even if only for a moment. What you see doesn't have to be what the next person sees.

Be curious. Let your imagination take over.
