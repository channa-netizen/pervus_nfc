01 / THE IDEA

WHY A CLOCK?

I've always been fascinated by the way time changes depending on what you're doing.

Five minutes can feel like an hour. An entire night can disappear in what feels like seconds. A song can pull you backward years. A moment with the right people can make everything else disappear completely.

So I built a clock that doesn't really give a shit what time it is.

Time Is an Illusion is meant to be touched, changed, played with, and experienced. There isn't a correct combination. There isn't a right way to look at it.

FUCK WITH IT.
MAKE IT YOURS FOR A MINUTE.

It looks like a grandfather clock got lost on the way home and wandered into a rave. Inside is a pile of modern electronics, custom controls, code, light, motion and questionable decisions.
