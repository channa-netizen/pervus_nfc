PERVUS

THE COSMIC NERVOUS FLOOF!

✨

In the weirdest part of space, a spark of anxious starlight came to life. That spark became Pervus, a fuzzy little blockhead made from cosmic static and leftover dreams. Puffy like a cloud, bright as the sun.

He’s our guardian of good vibes and nervous hearts, reminding everyone that it’s okay to be a little unsure.

ANXIETY CAN’T STOP THE VIBE!

He was sent to Earth to watch over those who dance through the chaos.

He don’t roar, he glows.
He don’t fight, he vibes.

And every light that shines from his fuzzy face whispers the same truth:

You’re doing great.
Even if you glow a little weird.
